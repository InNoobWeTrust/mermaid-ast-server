    subgraph Creation
        A[Create generation task] -->|Open dialog| B[Fill input]

        C -->|Free text| D[Free text input]
        C -->|Dropdown| E[Select option]
        D --> F[Input Parameters]
        E --> F
        F --> G[Submit Task]
        G --> |Create Task| H[Task]
        H --> I((Cancel Task))
    end

        H --> J[Run Task]
        J --> O[Agent]
        O --> K[Task result]
    end

    subgraph Validation
        K --> L[User Review]
        L --> |OK| M((Final Output))
        L --> |Not OK| N[Feedback]
        N --> H
    end
